================================================================================
CLAUD-TRADE BOT — COMPLETE OVERVIEW
================================================================================

You have $100,000 in a fake paper trading account (Alpaca). Nine bots trade it
automatically on GitHub's servers. No one has to touch anything — it runs
itself, fixes itself, and logs everything.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE 9 BOTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. TSLA TRAILING STOP
   Buys Tesla and holds it. Every time the price goes up, it moves a "stop"
   order up with it — like a floor under the price. If Tesla crashes, the floor
   triggers and sells automatically to lock in profits. Then it buys back in and
   starts again. Runs all day from 9:30 AM, relaunches at 2 PM as a backup.

2. WHEEL STRATEGY
   Sells options contracts on Tesla — basically selling insurance. Someone pays
   you cash upfront. You collect that premium over and over. If they force you to
   buy their shares, you immediately start selling covered calls to collect even
   more premium. Runs every 15 minutes during market hours.

3. COPY TRADER
   Looks up which US Congress members are making the most money on their stock
   trades (they are legally required to disclose this). Then copies those exact
   trades. The theory: politicians sometimes have early knowledge about which
   industries will benefit from upcoming laws. Runs hourly during market hours.

4. TJR STRATEGY
   Every morning 9:30 to 11 AM, watches SPY and QQQ for a specific pattern — a
   big price sweep that fakes traders out, followed by a reversal into a golden
   zone (50 to 79% retracement). Only enters if the reward is at least 1.5x the
   risk. High-frequency but selective.

5. MEAN REVERSION
   Every morning at 10 AM, scans stocks for ones beaten down way below their
   normal level (RSI under 30, below the Bollinger Band, 3 consecutive lower
   lows). Buys the beaten-down stock with a limit order below market. Sells the
   moment it bounces back up. Backtested 73% win rate over 9+ years — the most
   proven strategy in the system.

6. ORB — OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT
   Watches only QQQ (a tech ETF). The first 5 minutes of trading (9:30 to 9:35
   AM) sets a price range. If price then breaks ABOVE that range — buy. If it
   breaks BELOW — short sell. Holds all the way to 3:55 PM with no profit target
   so big trend days run fully. Only fires on high-volume days with real momentum.
   Relaunches at 2 PM as a backup.

7. SIP-ORB — STOCKS IN PLAY ORB
   Same breakout idea as ORB but does it on up to 10 stocks at once every
   morning. Scans 80+ stocks and picks the ones with unusually high volume in the
   first 5 minutes (means news or hype is driving them). Places breakout orders
   on all of them. Stop-loss sits at the opposite edge of the opening range.
   Closes everything by 3:55 PM. Up to 10 trades per day.

8. DUAL MOMENTUM
   Once a month, checks three things: Is the US stock market (SPY) beating cash?
   Is international stocks (EFA) beating US? Holds whichever is winning. If
   stocks are losing to cash, hides in bonds (AGG) instead. Like rotating into
   the best-performing asset class each month. Only about 1 to 2 trades per year.

9. DCA INDEX
   Every Monday, buys $500 of VOO (the entire US stock market in one ETF). Never
   sells. The boring-but-proven strategy — just keeps buying no matter what the
   price is. Builds a base that grows with the market over time.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SELF-IMPROVEMENT BOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Runs every day at 4:15 PM after the market closes.

   Step 1 — Pulls all the data: P&L for the day, open positions, error logs,
            trade history across every strategy.

   Step 2 — Sends everything to Claude AI and asks "what should we fix?"

   Step 3 — Claude writes actual code patches to improve the bots.

   Step 4 — Patches are automatically applied, but only if they pass two safety
            checks: no broken syntax, and no missing functions that would crash
            at runtime.

   Step 5 — Saves a full daily report to the repo with every trade, every
            decision, and every code change made.

The bots literally rewrite and improve themselves every single trading day.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PRE-MARKET SCANNER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Runs every day at 9:00 AM — 35 minutes before trading starts.

Scans 80 stocks, ranks which ones moved the hardest yesterday (those tend to
carry momentum into the next morning), and writes a ranked game plan before the
open. Commits it to the repo so there is a record of every morning's setup.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT ALL WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   GitHub's servers run everything for free
            |
            v
   Cron schedules trigger each bot at the right time
            |
            v
   Bots read and write their state to JSON files in the repo
            |
            v

            |
            v
   4:15 PM — self-improvement bot reviews the whole day
            |
            v
   Claude AI suggests fixes — patches auto-applied with safety gates
            |
            v
   Everything committed to GitHub with full history forever

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY RULES BAKED IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   - All bots use stop-limit orders, not plain stops — avoids wash-trade rejections
   - Self-improvement bot cannot edit itself — prevents breaking its own safety gates
   - Every code patch must pass syntax check + undefined-variable check before going live
   - All API keys live in GitHub secrets — never written in the code
   - State files save after every action so a crash mid-trade does not lose the position
   - Resting stop orders on Alpaca protect positions even if the GitHub job dies mid-day


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE GOAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Paper trade for 60 to 90 days. If the account grows and the daily reports show
consistent edge across multiple strategies, that is real evidence to decide
whether to switch to live money. The self-improvement loop means the system gets
smarter every single trading day automatically.
