### Problems Identified
1. **Wheel stop-loss is too wide**: 200% loss (current_price = 3× sell_price) is triggered at $7.95. The contract is at $6.84 and heading there. The strategy is absorbing large losses before acting.
2. **AMD limit sell expired**: Bot bought AMD at market then placed a limit sell that expired same-day, leaving an unintended long position.
3. **HWM not updated in tick()**: The monitor logs show HWM frozen at $408.42 even as TSLA trades between $389–$394. The HWM update logic appears absent from `tick()` — if price were to make a new high, it would be missed until restart.

LESSONS:
- The HWM in the trailing stop monitor was never updated during the tick loop — new intraday highs were silently ignored, meaning the trailing stop could never raise above where it was set at the last restart. Always update HWM in the tick body before computing stop levels.
- The wheel strategy's 200% stop-loss is too permissive for short puts on volatile underlyings like TSLA; a 100% loss (contract doubles) is a more appropriate exit before losses compound further toward assignment.
